5. How to Use MyVoice Studio

  1. Import your backing track.
  2. Optionally remove vocals and listen to both separation outputs.
  3. Choose the instrumental output to use it for karaoke.
  4. Load your lyrics and mark the start of each phrase.
  5. Record your performance while the music plays.
  6. Play back your take and download the recording or export an MP3.

Privacy: the selected song is processed in the browser by the separation library. The library and model are fetched from external hosts. This is not a guarantee that every browser component is offline or that your hosting provider receives no normal website traffic.
