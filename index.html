Automatic Voice Fixer

AUTOMATIC MODE
Vocal cleanup, compression, peak control, and an Auto-Tune-style pitch correction pass are enabled for export.

Pitch correction estimates the sung pitch and gently moves notes toward the nearest note in your selected key. It is a browser-based effect, not a full AI studio model; strong correction can sound robotic, and complex polyphonic or noisy recordings can confuse pitch detection.

Record a vocal, then preview or export the processed mix.
